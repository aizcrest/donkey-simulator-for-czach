Retrieved 2026-11-10 from the public Poly Pizza model pages and model-viewer files. Individual model pages record their licenses. Conversion used Blender 4.3.2; see `Tools/convert_models.py`. Animated source rigs/clips are retained where supplied. Bear and the Elk/Moose source are static meshes and do not include skeletal locomotion animations.

Quaternius pack information: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html

Original GLB files are not required at runtime. To reproduce conversion, download the `download` URLs in `Tools/AssetSources/models.json` as `<kind>.glb`, then run the converter with that folder. No paid asset store packages or glTF plugins are required.

The donkey, rider, terrain textures, grass, altar, gems and effects remain original project geometry. The image in `Docs/forest-rider-concept.png` is generated concept artwork, not gameplay.
