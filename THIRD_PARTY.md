Original seven models were retrieved 2026-11-10; Donkey and Knight/Rider were added 2026-11-12. License metadata comes from each linked public model page. Copies of the CC BY 3.0 and CC0 legal texts are in `Licenses/`.

Conversion used Blender 4.3.2. `Tools/AssetSources/models.json` records download URLs and converted FBX hashes. Download the GLB files as `<kind>.glb`, then run `blender -b --python Tools/convert_models.py -- SOURCE_DIRECTORY`. To convert selected models only, append `--only Rider Donkey`. The rider adaptation and four original mounted clips are generated by `Tools/mounted_rider.py`. Runtime Unity import selects/reduces the clips actually used.

Quaternius animal pack information: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html

The earlier segmented models generated by `Tools/create_mounted_models.py` and the Donkey/Knight FBXs listed above remain as legacy reference assets; the active v2 models are credited below. Saddle, barding, sword, low-poly forest props, terrain textures, grass, altar/gems and effects are original project geometry. Bear and Moose source meshes are static; other included model files contain animation clips.

## Original audio

`Tools/generate_audio.py` creates the 19 mono WAV files in `Assets/Resources/Audio`. Effects, ambience and the short music loop are original synthesized project work, with no external sample recordings or third-party music license. It requires numpy when regenerating; Unity does not require Python or numpy to use the included WAVs.

The image `Docs/forest-rider-concept.png` is generated concept artwork, not a gameplay screenshot.

`Assets/Resources/Branding/CzachsDonkeyJourney.png` and `Assets/Art/AppIcon/DonkeyKnight.png` are AI-generated title/splash and launcher artwork created for this project on 2026-11-14. They are illustrations, not gameplay screenshots, and use no downloaded third-party source images.

## Precomputed enemy animation frames

`Assets/Resources/EnemyFrames` contains pose snapshots derived from the listed Quaternius Deer, Wolf, Fox, Zombie and Dragon assets under CC0. `Tools/bake_enemy_frames.py` samples their source animations in Blender, normalizes the geometry and bakes material/vertex/texture colors into per-vertex colors. Source FBXs and license records remain included. Bear/Moose retain their static FBX rendering.

## Tutorial snapshots

`Assets/Resources/Tutorial/{ride,combat,orbs,dragon}.png` are staged instructional renders made with `Tools/render_tutorial_snapshots.py`, not gameplay screenshots. They use the credited Polyfork donkey (CC BY 4.0), KayKit knight/equipment (CC0), and Quaternius wolf/dragon (CC0), alongside original forest props, altar, markers and orbs. The donkey's credit, source link, license and adaptation notice appear above and in Camp → Asset Credits.

The enemy attack update resamples those same CC0 animation clips with 12 attack poses including the endpoint. Runtime pose pairs interpolate the baked positions and normals. Bear/moose keep their existing credited meshes and receive original procedural body-motion cues; their source assets and licenses are unchanged.
